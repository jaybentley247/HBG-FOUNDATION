The Process

How it works

1
Apply online

Fill in our short application with your details, the grant type and the amount you need.

2
We review

Our team reads every application carefully and may contact you for more information.

3
Get a decision

You'll receive an email letting you know whether your grant has been approved.

4
Receive funds

Approved grants are typically disbursed within 5–7 days.

Who can apply

  • Adults aged 18 or older
  • Residents of the United States
  • Individuals facing financial hardship, students, or small-business owners
  • Applicants who can clearly explain how the grant will be used

What to have ready

  • Contact details and home address
  • The amount you're requesting and what it's for
  • A short description of your situation
  • Supporting documents if you have them (bills, invoices, enrollment letters)
